# Privacy Policy for NearTransfer

**Last Updated: December 2025**

## Introduction

NearTransfer ("we", "our", or "the App") is a peer-to-peer file sharing application that allows users to transfer files, contacts, messages, and other data between devices connected to the same local WiFi network. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data.

## Data Collection and Usage

### What We DON'T Collect
- **Your Files**: We never upload, store, or access your files on any external servers. All file transfers occur directly between your devices over your local network.
- **Personal Information**: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any personally identifiable information.
- **Message Content**: SMS messages transferred via Phone Clone are sent directly to your other device and are never stored on our servers.
- **Call History**: Call logs transferred via Phone Clone are processed locally and never leave your local network.

### What We May Collect
- **Anonymous Analytics**: Basic app usage statistics (feature usage, crash reports) to improve the app experience. This can be disabled in Settings.
- **Advertising Data**: Our ad partner (Google AdMob) may collect advertising identifiers for personalized ads. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

## Permissions We Request

### Required Permissions
| Permission | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| Internet | For signaling server connection to establish peer-to-peer transfers |
| WiFi/Network State | To discover nearby devices on your local network |
| Storage | To read files you want to share and save received files |

### Optional Permissions
| Permission | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| Camera | QR code scanning for quick device pairing |
| Contacts | Contact sharing feature and Phone Clone backup |
| **Call Logs** | Phone Clone feature - backup and restore call history to a new device |
| **SMS Messages** | Phone Clone feature - backup and restore text messages to a new device |
| Phone State | Required to access call log metadata |
| Photos & Videos | Media gallery access for sharing and Phone Clone backup |

### Important Notice About SMS and Call Log Permissions
We understand these are sensitive permissions. Here's how we handle them:

1. **Local Processing Only**: All SMS and Call Log data is processed entirely on your device
2. **Direct Transfer**: Data is transmitted directly to your receiving device over your local WiFi network
3. **No Cloud Storage**: This data is NEVER uploaded to any external servers
4. **User Initiated**: These features only activate when you explicitly use Phone Clone
5. **Optional**: You can deny these permissions and still use all other app features

## Data Transfer

All data transfers in NearTransfer occur:
- **Peer-to-Peer**: Directly between your devices
- **Local Network Only**: Over your WiFi network, not through the internet
- **Encrypted**: Using WebRTC DTLS encryption
- **No Intermediary**: Without passing through our servers

## Third-Party Services

### Google AdMob
We display advertisements through Google AdMob. Google may collect:
- Advertising ID
- Device information
- General location (country/region)

You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings. For more information, see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### In-App Purchases
We use Google Play Billing for premium subscriptions. Purchase data is handled by Google according to their privacy policy.

## Data Security

- All peer-to-peer connections use **DTLS encryption** (WebRTC standard)
- We do not store any user data on external servers
- File transfers happen directly between devices without intermediaries

## Children's Privacy

NearTransfer is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Deny Permissions**: Refuse any optional permission without losing core functionality
- **Disable Analytics**: Turn off anonymous usage statistics in Settings
- **Opt-Out of Personalized Ads**: Through your device's advertising settings
- **Delete Data**: Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored app data

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date.

## Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the App's feedback feature in Settings.
